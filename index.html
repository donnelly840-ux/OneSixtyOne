About

Bespoke brand strategy, design, and web for the people who care about the details.

You're not a client to us.
You're a partner.

OneSixtyOne is a small, founder-led creative practice. We do brand strategy and design built around where you are and where you're headed. Nothing off the shelf.

Our small, trusted network of collaborators lets us build the right team for each partner, each challenge and each creative direction. Close enough to stay focused, with the range to flex when a project calls for it. Shared design principles, high standards and people who genuinely love doing the work well.

How we work

The pillars that shape our approach.

01

No templates.

Every project starts from scratch. We don't pull from a library of past work and swap the logo. Your brief gets a fresh answer, built for you.

02

No layers.

The person you brief is the person doing the work. No account managers relaying your feedback through a game of telephone.

03

No shortcuts.

Spacing, rhythm, the right word in the right place. The details that go unnoticed are usually doing the most work.

04

No BS.

Timelines, budgets, what's working and what isn't. We'd rather have an uncomfortable conversation early than a costly one later.

Brand DNA

Every brand has a code.
We help you find yours.

Brand DNA is what holds a brand together: purpose, voice, positioning, and visual identity. When it's defined, decisions get cleaner. When it's not, things drift.

We map it from the ground up.
